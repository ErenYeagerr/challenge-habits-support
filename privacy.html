Privacy Policy

Challenge Habits helps small groups run habit challenges together. This policy explains what information the app uses, why it uses it, and how account deletion works.

Information We Collect

Challenge Habits collects your chosen display name, account identifier, challenge names, prize descriptions, invite codes, participant lists, selected creature or egg, selected habits, readiness state, and check-in status. Group chat messages, abuse reports, and blocked-user preferences are stored to provide chat and safety features. Authentication providers may process account information supplied during sign-in.

If you allow notifications, the app stores push notification tokens and notification preferences. The app records limited product interaction, error, and performance events associated with your account identifier and app version to diagnose problems and improve reliability. These events do not include habit text, chat text, prize descriptions, or invite codes. Sentry crash reporting is disabled in this release.

Liked quotes are stored locally on your device. Support requests contain the information you choose to send, such as your email address, device details, and description of the issue.

Challenge Habits should not be used to store sensitive health, financial, legal, or emergency information.

How We Use Information

We use information to create and maintain your account, show group progress, let invited participants join the correct challenge, send reminders when enabled, diagnose bugs, improve reliability, provide support, and support account deletion.

What Other Participants Can See

People in the same challenge can see your display name, selected habits, selected creature, readiness state, daily completion status, challenge progress, and group chat messages. Invite codes and invite links are meant to be shared only with people you want in the challenge.

Service Providers

Challenge Habits uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, and backend functions. Apple services process information related to Sign in with Apple, TestFlight, App Store downloads, and device notifications. Support email is handled through Gmail. The public support and policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose servers may process standard request information such as IP addresses.

Data We Do Not Sell

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. The app is free and does not process wagers, money pools, prize payments, redemptions, or financial transfers.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Challenge Habits may store a push notification token for your device and use it to send habit reminders or challenge status notifications. You can disable notifications in iOS Settings or inside the app where notification settings are available.

Account Deletion

You can request account deletion from the Account area inside the app. Some records may remain temporarily in backups or logs for security, reliability, fraud prevention, legal, or operational reasons, then expire according to the service provider's retention practices.

Children

Challenge Habits is intended for general audiences using App Store account controls. Children should use the app only with parent or guardian permission.

Security

We use authentication, row-level access rules, and backend validation to protect challenge data. No app or internet service can guarantee perfect security, so invite links and challenge content should be shared thoughtfully.
