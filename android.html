Challenge Habits for Android

Version 1.0.1. Android 8.0 or newer.

Download Android app

Free direct download. APK, approximately 19 MB.

Install

  1. Download the APK to your Android phone and open it.
  2. If Android asks, allow this browser to install this app, then return to the installer.
  3. Open Challenge Habits and enter your display name.
  4. Tap the plus button, choose Join with invite code, and enter the code shared by your challenge creator.

Keep Play Protect enabled. If you see a security warning or the installer refuses the app, stop and contact support rather than disabling protection. You can turn off the browser's install permission after installation.

Updates

Install future APK updates over the existing app. Do not uninstall or clear app storage: your account and locally saved quotes are stored on this device. If Android says an update is incompatible, contact support before changing anything.

This is a direct installation, not a Google Play download. Updates are manual. Moving to a future Google Play release may require additional steps; do not uninstall to switch.

Reminders

Daily reminders are optional in Settings. Allow notifications when prompted. Delivery time may vary with Android battery settings. Group progress and chat refresh while the app is open; this version does not send background chat push notifications.
