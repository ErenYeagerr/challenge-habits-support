Support

Need help with Challenge Habits? Start with the quick checks below.

Fast Checks

  • Make sure you are on the newest TestFlight or App Store build.
  • Force quit and reopen the app if a screen appears stale.
  • Check that your device has an internet connection.
  • If an invite code fails, ask the challenge creator to share the code again.
  • If notifications are missing, check iOS Settings > Notifications > Challenge Habits.

What To Include

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, build number, what you were trying to do, the approximate time the issue happened, your timezone, any diagnostic code shown in the app, and a screenshot if it helps.

Do not send passwords, private Apple account information, payment details, or sensitive health information.

Beta Feedback

During TestFlight beta, the best support path is TestFlight feedback because it automatically includes useful build and device details.

Contact Support

Email onsbons3@gmail.com for help, feedback, privacy questions, or account deletion assistance.

Report Abuse

Use the report or block action on a group chat message to report content or hide a sender's messages. You can also email support with the challenge name and approximate time of the message. Do not include private invite codes or passwords.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account from the Account area inside the app. If deletion fails, contact support with your app build number, approximate time of the attempt, and any diagnostic code shown.

Prize And Group Agreements

Challenge Habits does not manage payments, wagers, prize pools, redemptions, or enforcement. Prizes are informal text agreed on by the challenge group.
